## ⚠️ COMMON QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

### **Q: "Why does this need federal funding?"**
**A:** Legal aid is federally funded through LSC and HHS grants. Technology to serve legal aid must be accessible to all, not just wealthy law firms. Federal funding ensures equity and nationwide access. Plus, the ROI to taxpayers is 20:1 to 50:1 through foster care savings.

### **Q: "Why not just hire more attorneys?"**
**A:** We should! But even with more attorneys, they need tools. One attorney with this platform can serve 2x as many families as one without it. It's force multiplication, not replacement.

### **Q: "Is AI reliable for legal work?"**
**A:** AI is a tool, not a replacement for human judgment. Attorneys review all AI outputs. Think of it like Westlaw or legal research databases—helpful tools that require attorney expertise to use properly.

### **Q: "What about privacy and security?"**
**A:** Bank-level encryption, SOC 2 compliance path, WCAG accessibility. We're building to government security standards from day one.

### **Q: "Why can't private market solve this?"**
**A:** Legal aid market is too small for big tech companies to prioritize. But the social need is enormous. This requires public-private partnership—federal funding for initial deployment, then sustainable subscription model.

### **Q: "How do you make money?"**
**A:** After federal pilot proves the model, we transition to subscriptions: legal aid organizations and government agencies pay $79-$299/attorney/month. Free tier for self-represented parents. Sustainable by Year 4.

### **Q: "Why you? What's your background?"**
**A:** [Be honest about your background. Emphasize: passion for helping families, technology expertise, commitment to access to justice. If you lack legal background, mention legal advisors/partnerships.]
