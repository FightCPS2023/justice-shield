## 🏛️ GOVERNMENT CONTRACT TYPES

### **1. GSA Schedule Contract**
**Benefit:** Pre-approved pricing for federal agencies  
**Process:** 6-12 months to establish  
**Value:** Opens door to all federal agencies  
**Discount:** Typically 10-15% off commercial pricing

### **2. State Master Service Agreement (MSA)**
**Benefit:** Pre-qualified vendor for state agencies  
**Process:** 3-6 months per state  
**Value:** Streamlined procurement for state entities  
**Term:** 3-5 years with optional renewals

### **3. Cooperative Purchase Contract**
**Benefit:** One contract used by multiple agencies  
**Examples:** NASPO, NCPA, Sourcewell  
**Value:** Exponential reach with single contract  
**Term:** 5-10 years

### **4. Direct Agency Contract**
**Benefit:** Customized solution for specific agency  
**Process:** RFP response, negotiations  
**Value:** Highest customization, premium pricing  
**Term:** 1-5 years with renewals
